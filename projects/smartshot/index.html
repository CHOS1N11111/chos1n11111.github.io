Overview

SmartShot is a native macOS utility that brings smart content-block selection, region and scrolling screenshots, image annotation, on-device OCR, privacy-aware history, and screen recording into one app. Capture processing stays on the Mac, with no account, analytics, or cloud upload service. Its browser extension can capture webpage content blocks and hand the resulting image to the native editor. The project targets macOS 14 and later and is currently available as a 0.2.2 preview.
