Overview

DongqiudiPure Android is an unofficial Dongqiudi client that brings football news, fixtures, results, and competition statistics into a native Android interface. Readers can open articles with embedded media and comments, inspect match lineups and events, and move from league standings to team and player profiles. Custom news columns, competition selection, and locally saved preferences support a focused browsing experience, with light and dark themes and no advertising or betting content. Built with Kotlin and Jetpack Compose, the project remains in development.
