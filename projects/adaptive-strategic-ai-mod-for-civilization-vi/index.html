Overview

Adaptive Strategic AI is a Gathering Storm AI overhaul designed to keep Deity games competitive from the opening through the victory screen. It replaces much of vanilla Deity's front-loaded spike with an era-scaled difficulty curve, while each major AI independently adjusts development, recovery, expansion, defense, pressure, and war plans based on its position and recent trend. The mod adjusts AI priorities and bonuses to improve settlement, economy, military readiness, city assaults, wartime production, and victory progress, while limiting recovery bonuses to AIs with confirmed broad or severe deficits. City production decisions and military actions remain controlled by the game's native AI. It does not spawn units or grant technologies, civics, resources, cities, or stored progress.
