Overview

Formula 1 Data Analysis and Prediction is a reproducible end-to-end pipeline for historical analysis and 2026 championship forecasting. It acquires and validates race data, builds analysis and model-ready tables, explores driver, constructor, and circuit patterns, compares models for podium, Top 10, and race-points prediction, calibrates probabilities, and uses backtesting and simulation to produce driver and constructor championship forecasts. Static historical figures cover 2019–2025, while the final forecast uses the current 2026 season state.
